The red lip edit: 7 reds that start conversations

Blue-red, orange-red, brick and cherry, from the drugstore to Dior.

after 10pm

A red lip walks in before you do. That’s the point.

Red has a reputation for being hard to wear. It isn’t. It’s just specific. The right red depends on your undertone, your mood and how late you plan to stay out. Blue-reds run cool and bright. Orange-reds run warm and loud. Brick is the grown-up red, made for rust, camel and gold. Cherry is pure play. A stain is red that looks like it grew there.

Finish matters as much as shade. Matte stays put and means business. Satin moves with you and catches the light. A stain survives dinner, drinks and whatever happens after.

So here are seven reds across every undertone and finish, priced from the drugstore to Dior. Find the one that sounds like you on your loudest day. Then wear it on a Tuesday.

How to wear red like you mean it

Prep first: a swipe of balm, blotted off, then colour. Line with a pencil close to your shade, or use the edge of the bullet for a softer shape. Apply, blot, apply again for colour that lasts. Let the lip lead and keep everything else polished: groomed brows, mascara, a little glow. Tuck the lipstick and a tissue in your bag for touch-ups. Cool undertones, start with blue-red. Warm, go orange-red or brick. Not sure yet? Start with a stain and build it up until it feels like you.

Red isn’t a risk. It’s a statement. Say it first.
