Black lace, red lip: the after-dark edit

Seven pieces, one loud night: black lace, sharp shoulders, satin, and red shoes.

after 10pm

Some nights call for a quiet dinner. This is not that edit.

This is the look for the birthday that turns into a story, the gallery opening you arrive late to on purpose, the dinner where somebody orders the second bottle. It starts with black lace and a red lip and builds from there: a blazer with real shoulders, a satin skirt that moves when you do, sheer black tights with a little shine, and red satin heels that make the whole thing impossible to ignore.

Seven pieces, and every one earns its spot. The lace is the mood. The blazer is the authority. The heels are the punctuation. Gold earrings and a crystal bag that holds a powder and a lipstick, and not your phone, finish it off, because tonight you are not answering emails.

Wear all seven at once, or steal two for something already in your closet. Either way, walk in like you were expected, and stay until they turn the lights up.

Putting it together:

  • Tuck the lace. The blouse goes into the satin skirt; the blazer goes on open, or over the shoulders.
  • Pick one red. Red shoes, red lip. Keep everything else black and gold so the red reads as intention.
  • Let the tights do the weather. Sheer black takes a satin skirt from September into a Toronto November without losing the shine.
  • Earrings up, hair back. Statement earrings need room. Give them a sleek bun or a hard side part.

You don’t need a reason to dress like this. The night is the reason.
