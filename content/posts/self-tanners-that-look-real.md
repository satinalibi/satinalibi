A real-looking tan isn't about going darker. It's about going golden on your terms, on your schedule, with no sun required.

The old fears are fair. Streaky ankles. Orange palms. That biscuit smell following you into the meeting. Formulas have moved on. Today's self-tanners come as drops you mix into moisturizer, mousses that dry in minutes, creams that build slowly, mists made for your face, and wash-off glow for when the party is tonight.

We picked one for every way you might want to tan. Some commit for days. One rinses off before breakfast. All of them are about colour and radiance, never about fixing anything, because nothing needed fixing.

Pick your format, prep your skin, and let the bronze do the talking. It has a lot to say.

<!-- products -->

## How to make it look real

Exfoliate the day before, not the hour before. Moisturize elbows, knees, ankles and the sides of your hands so colour doesn't grab there. Use a mitt for mousse, and wash your palms straight after anything you apply by hand. Build in thin layers: two light coats beat one heavy one every time. With face drops or mist, blend down onto your neck and ears so everything matches. Keep the colour fresh between rounds with a gradual cream.

One honest note: self-tan isn't sunscreen. Wear your SPF anyway.

Bronze is a choice. Make it loudly.
