There's glowing, and there's glowing loud. This list is the second one.

A good shimmer oil has one job: make the light land on you and stay there. Not glitter that migrates to everyone you hug. Not a greasy sheen that ruins your silk. Just skin that looks lit from somewhere warm, whether you're under a patio heater or a harsh bar spotlight.

We went looking for the ones worth your money. Six come in under $50, from a drugstore gel oil that punches far above its price to a French cult classic dipped in gold. One is a splurge, because some nights call for it.

Every pick here is about finish, scent and how it wears. Nothing here is about changing you. Your skin is already the point. These just turn up the volume.

Shake, pump, smooth it on, and go be seen.

## How to wear it loud

Apply to clean, dry skin and give it a few minutes to settle before you get dressed, especially under silk or satin. Go where the light hits first: shoulders, collarbones, the front of your shins. Smooth a little over the backs of your hands, because you talk with them. For a face glow, stick to the oils made for face and body, and tap over makeup instead of rubbing. Wear shimmer with one gold piece and let nothing else compete. Want more? Layer a tinted oil over a fresh self-tan and watch people lose their train of thought.

You were never meant to blend in. Glow like it's on purpose.
