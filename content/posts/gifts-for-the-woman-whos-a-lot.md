So skip the candle. Give her something she'll wear loudly: pearls with a little swing, a red that means business, gold big enough to catch the light from across the room. Everything here is under $100 USD, and nothing here is quiet.

## How to give it

Don't wrap it in tissue and apologize for it. Hand it over at dinner, in front of everyone, and tell her it reminded you of her. It did.

And if the woman who's a lot is you? Buy it anyway. Nobody deserves it more.
