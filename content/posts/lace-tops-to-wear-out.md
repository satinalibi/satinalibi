Somebody decided lace belongs in a drawer. We disagree, loudly.

Lace is just fabric. Gorgeous, complicated, slightly dangerous fabric, and it looks its best in daylight, under a blazer, in a room full of people about to hear your idea. The trick isn't hiding it. The trick is pairing it with something that means business: sharp trousers, a boxy jacket, a pencil skirt, a good bag.

So here are seven lace tops built to be worn out. Camis with lace trim that sit under a suit. A corset top that stretches like a tank. A cotton lace button-down with an actual collar. A long-sleeve Chantilly lace bodysuit. They run from $48 to $195, and every one of them works on a Tuesday.

Nothing here needs permission. Wear it to the meeting. Wear it to lunch after the meeting. If anyone raises an eyebrow, raise yours back.

<!-- products -->

**How to wear lace to work without asking anyone's opinion:**

- **Anchor it.** Lace plus tailoring is the whole formula. Wide-leg trousers, a straight blazer, a midi pencil skirt.
- **Plan the layer underneath.** Under semi-sheer pieces, wear a smooth cami or bra in black or close to your skin tone, on purpose.
- **Keep one thing sharp.** A pointed flat, a structured bag, a slick bun. The lace gets to be the soft part.
- **Let it peek.** A lace-trim cami under a buttoned blazer shows an inch of edge. An inch is plenty.

Lace was never the risk. Staying quiet was.
