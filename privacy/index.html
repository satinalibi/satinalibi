Privacy

What happens to your information when you visit Satin Alibi.

Last updated: September 2026

We don’t ask for personal information. Satin Alibi has no accounts, no comment forms, and no newsletter sign-up right now. If that changes, this page will change first.

Hosting. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes basic technical information (like IP address and browser type) to deliver pages and keep the site secure. We may look at anonymous, combined visit numbers (for example, how many people read a post) to understand what’s working.

Affiliate links. When you click a product link, the retailer or affiliate network (such as ShopMy or Amazon) may set cookies so they can tell that your purchase came from Satin Alibi. That tracking happens on their sites and follows their privacy policies.

Pinterest. If you arrive from Pinterest or save something to Pinterest, Pinterest’s privacy policy applies to what happens on Pinterest. If we add Pinterest’s measurement tag in future, it will measure visits and purchases from Pinterest ads, and we’ll list it here.

Your choices. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking cookies won’t stop the site from working.

Contact. Questions about privacy go to hello@satinalibi.com.
