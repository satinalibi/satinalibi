Gifts for the woman who’s a lot

Nine gifts under $100 for the friend who laughs the loudest, orders dessert first and has never once whispered.

none of them quiet

Some women are easy to buy for. She isn’t. She already owns the black dress, the good perfume and a strong opinion on both.

So skip the candle. Give her something she’ll wear loudly: pearls with a little swing, a red that means business, gold big enough to catch the light from across the room. Everything here is under $100 USD, and nothing here is quiet.
