Affiliate disclosure

The short version: we might earn a commission, and it never changes what we pick.

Satin Alibi is supported by affiliate links. When you click a product link on this site and buy something, the retailer or an affiliate network (such as ShopMy, and in some cases Amazon) may pay us a small commission. The price you pay stays the same.

A few promises:

  • We only feature things we’d genuinely recommend. A commission never buys a spot on this site.
  • Affiliate links are marked. Any post with affiliate links carries a note near the top.
  • No paid posts in disguise. If a brand ever sponsors something, we’ll say so clearly at the top of the post.
  • Prices change. The prices we list were correct when we checked them. Always check the retailer before you buy.

This disclosure follows the advertising guidelines of Canada’s Competition Bureau and the U.S. Federal Trade Commission, which ask creators to be clear when there’s a financial relationship.
